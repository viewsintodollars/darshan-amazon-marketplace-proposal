The marketplace journey

Five things have to be true before a shopper buys

Every recommendation in this proposal attaches to one of these five steps. If a step is broken, spending more on media only buys more traffic for a page that does not convert.

Build a clean catalogue foundation, create repeatable listing and visual systems, then expand only after search terms, conversion, inventory and contribution margin are visible at ASIN level.

Seller Central, Brand Registry, advertising, inventory, pricing, returns and ASIN history were not available. Those items are defined as discovery requirements in this proposal.

Operating model

Five systems, run as one

The engagement is not a content project with ads attached. Catalogue accuracy, content, retail readiness, media and analytics operate on one loop, and a failure in any one of them shows up as wasted spend in another.

CatalogueCorrect facts and relationships
ContentSearch and conversion assets
RetailPrice, stock, fulfilment, reviews
MediaDemand capture and defence
AnalyticsProfit and incrementality

What this prevents

  • Media funding ASINs that are out of stock or losing the Buy Box
  • Content rewrites that are undone by a third-party catalogue contribution
  • Rank built on a listing that is suppressed a month later

What it produces

  • One verified ASIN register that every team works from
  • Reusable listing and A plus families, not one-off pages
  • Contribution margin visible per ASIN, after media

Variation policy

Incorrect variation families are the fastest way to damage a working catalogue

A parent family concentrates reviews and traffic when it is correct, and destroys relevance when it is not. The rules below are applied before any listing is built.

Use a parent family only where the category permits itEvery child must be a genuine version of the same core product, under a variation theme the category actually supports.
Keep formats and formulas separateDifferent formats, formulas, bundles and materially different pack contents stay as separate families unless Amazon policy explicitly permits the relationship.
Put facts in attributes, not in proseSize, weight, count and fragrance belong in the correct structured attributes. Essential differences are never hidden in images or free text.
One approved naming dictionaryA single agreed vocabulary for Darshan, product lines, fragrances, formats and pack expressions, applied everywhere.
Naming and pack clarity were the two highest-rated risks in the public audit. Both are resolved by the dictionary and the variation rules above, before a single asset is produced.
