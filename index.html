Two findings carry immediate commercial risk

High · Naming

Public catalogues use Darshan, Darshan Incense and Darshan Fragrance

Spellings vary between agarbatti and aggarbati. Brand and product naming inconsistency splits search relevance and confuses customers.

High · Pack clarity

Reseller titles sometimes combine units and sets inconsistently

11
Variation policy

Incorrect variation families are the fastest way to damage a working catalogue

A parent family concentrates reviews and traffic when it is correct, and destroys relevance when it is not.

Rule 01

Use a parent family only where the category permits it

Every child must be a genuine version of the same core product, under a variation theme the category actually supports.

Rule 02

Keep formats and formulas separate

Different formats, formulas, bundles and materially different pack contents stay as separate families unless Amazon policy explicitly permits the relationship.

Rule 03

Put facts in attributes, not in prose

Size, weight, count and fragrance belong in structured attributes. Essential differences are never hidden in images or free text.

Rule 04

One approved naming dictionary

A single agreed vocabulary for Darshan, product lines, fragrances, formats and pack expressions, applied everywhere.

Pack discipline

One pack weight per title. Never mix variants.

Bharath Darshan

Darshan Bharath Darshan Incense Sticks, 90 g

Keyword focus: bharath darshan agarbatti, Darshan incense sticks, puja agarbatti, incense for home.

The constraint

Use "90 g" only when it matches the exact consumer pack

Do not mix the 14 g, 30 g, 82 g and 90 g variants in one title or child ASIN. Each is a separate sellable unit with its own identifier.

Pack clarity was one of the two highest-rated risks in the public audit. This rule is how it gets closed.

30
Reading the numbers

Two metrics that mislead if read alone

A low ACOS

Can still hide stalled organic growth

Efficient spend on branded terms looks excellent while non-branded rank goes nowhere. The brand pays to reach customers it already had.

A high ROAS

Can still be unprofitable

Once referral fees, fulfilment, returns, discounts and landed cost are included, a strong-looking return can sit below break-even.

Therefore
Set targets after the baseline, and report ASIN-level contribution and total advertising cost of sales alongside every sales metric.
